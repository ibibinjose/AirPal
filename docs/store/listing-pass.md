## Full Description (App Store & Google Play)

```text
AirPal Pass is your all-in-one passbook for live concerts, cultural festivals, ticketed experiences, and exclusive member dining perks.

Never dig through your emails for a PDF ticket again. AirPal Pass gives you immediate, offline-ready access to all your confirmed tickets, Apple Wallet passes, and venue perks in one secure pocket hub.

DISCOVER LIVE CONCERTS & CULTURAL FESTIVALS
• Browse upcoming live music, cultural gatherings, tech mixers, and community events.
• View live attendee previews, venue details, tier allocations, and artist schedules.
• Reserve and purchase tickets securely via Apple Pay and native Stripe checkout in seconds.

DYNAMIC QR PASSBOOK & 1-TAP APPLE WALLET
• Store all your event tickets and vouchers in a dedicated offline-friendly passbook.
• Export any pass directly into Apple Wallet (.pkpass) or Google Wallet with 1 tap.
• Live rotating dynamic QR codes guarantee fast gate entry and protect against duplicate scans.

ULTRA-FAST DOOR CHECK-IN SCANNER
• Running an event? Switch into Door Scanner mode in a single tap.
• Scan attendee tickets and QR passes in milliseconds using your device’s camera.
• Works with intermittent connectivity: decodes tickets on-device and auto-syncs door capacity and guestlists.

MEMBER PERKS & LOCAL MERCHANT DEALS
• Unlock exclusive restaurant dining discounts, complimentary drinks, and partner perks.
• Flash your verified dynamic redemption pass to claim perks on the spot at participating venues.
• Track your savings and reward vouchers in your active deals ledger.

ORGANISER STUDIO
• Create events, configure standard/VIP/group tiers, and monitor live door statistics.
• Manage staff scanner permissions and broadcast push updates directly to ticket holders.

Download AirPal Pass — Your Access to Experiences.
```
