```text
AirPal Pass is your all-in-one passbook for live concerts, cultural festivals, ticketed experiences, and member merchant perks.

DISCOVER LIVE EVENTS & EXPERIENCES
Browse concerts, cultural festivals, tech mixers, and community gatherings in your city. Inspect live attendee previews and reserve tickets in seconds.

DYNAMIC QR PASSBOOK & 1-TAP WALLET
Store and access all your event passes in one fast, offline-ready passbook. Export passes with 1 tap directly to Apple Wallet (.pkpass) and Google Wallet. Live dynamic QR codes prevent duplicate gate entry.

FAST DOOR CHECK-IN SCANNER
Organisers and door teams can scan tickets and walk-in guests in milliseconds. The high-speed camera scanner decodes tickets offline and automatically synchronizes door rosters with Cloud Functions.

Claim member dining perks, drink vouchers, and venue discounts. Flash your verified digital voucher card to redeem on the spot with local merchants.

ORGANISER STUDIO & GUESTLIST
Organisers can manage ticket allocations, monitor real-time door guestlists, create discount promo codes, and broadcast updates to ticket holders.

Download AirPal Pass — Your Access to Experiences.
```
